PROMPT ID: P3 — Output Review
Workflow task: Review a generated advisory against its original forecast.
Purpose: Identify unsupported claims, omissions, hidden uncertainty and unsafe framing.
Implementation note: This prompt sheet documents the review task. The deployed Python prototype's automatic reviewer is heuristic and is not an LLM reviewer.
User template:
Original forecast: {forecast_text}
Generated advisory: {advisory_text}
Review for invented/unsupported claims, changes or omissions of forecast facts, missing/uncertain information, unsupported audience assumptions and responsible communication. Recommend corrections.
Output: Overall Review; Unsupported Claims; Missing or Uncertain Information; Recommended Corrections; Final Verdict (Accept/Revise/Reject) with justification.
Variables: forecast_text is the source; advisory_text is the candidate being reviewed.
